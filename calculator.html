Reading the result

  • Landed vs FOB markup — under ~25–35% is healthy for sea freight at typical MOQs. Over 50% usually means: freight quote too high, order too small, or duty rate worse than assumed.
  • The 2026 reality — the $800 de minimis exemption is gone. Every commercial import pays duty; small "test orders" that used to dodge fees now don't. That's why verification before ordering matters more than ever: verify before you wire.
  • Fees you can't negotiate vs can — duty and VAT are fixed by code and value. Freight is negotiable (three quotes minimum). FOB price is negotiable — that's the Playbook's territory.
