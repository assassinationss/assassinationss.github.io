How to read the score

  • 80–100 · Looks clean on paper. Still get the documents before the wire: license, PI, and account name must match exactly. Clean checklists have been faked; cross-checked documents rarely are.
  • 50–79 · Proceed with caution. The failed items are your pre-wire checklist. Each one is fixable — a real supplier clears them in days; a shell stalls for weeks.
